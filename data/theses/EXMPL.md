# What I think is being missed

This is the whole point of the site. Not "the company is good" — good companies are
priced as good companies. The claim has to be that a *specific* fact is not yet in the
price, and a reason that fact is being overlooked: it sits in a segment nobody models
separately, it shows up two quarters from now, the sell-side covers it under the wrong
sector, the float is too small for the funds that would otherwise own it.

> The strongest version of a pre-consensus call names the date the consensus changes.

# The numbers

- What the security is worth if you're right, and roughly when
