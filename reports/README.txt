PDF reports go in this folder.

Reference them from data/portfolio.json on the relevant position:

  "reports": [
    { "title": "NEU / AMPAC — full write-up",
      "date": "2026-08-26",
      "file": "reports/NEU-2026-08.pdf" }
  ]

Export Word documents to PDF before putting them here. Browsers display PDFs
natively; a .docx just downloads and needs Word to open.
